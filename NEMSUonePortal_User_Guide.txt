[PUBLIC] — 0.1  VISITING THE PORTAL (LANDING PAGE)
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  The landing page is the public homepage of the portal. You
  can browse campus information, view academic programs, read
  announcements, and find links to sign in or sign up.

HOW TO USE IT
  Step 1. Open your web browser and go to the portal address
          provided by the institution.
  Step 2. Scroll down to explore the following sections:
          - About the Campus
          - Vision and Mission
          - Academic Programs offered
          - Campus Facilities
          - Latest Announcements
  Step 3. To go to the login page, click "Sign In" in the
          top navigation bar.
  Step 4. To create a new account, click "Sign Up" or
          the "Enroll Now" button in the hero section.

NOTES
  - The landing page is public — no login is required to
    view it.
  - Announcements shown here are visible to everyone.


----------------------------------------------------------------
[PUBLIC] — 0.2  CREATING YOUR ACCOUNT (SIGN UP)
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  This page lets approved applicants create their student
  account. You will need your pre-enrollment reference number
  provided by the registrar's office.

HOW TO USE IT
  Step 1. Click "Sign Up" on the landing page or login page.
  Step 2. STEP 1 OF 4 — Enter your Reference Number.
          Type the pre-enrollment ID or reference number
          given to you by the registrar's office.
          Click "Look Up".
  Step 3. STEP 2 OF 4 — Confirm your details.
          Your name and program will appear on screen.
          If the information is correct, click "Continue".
  Step 4. STEP 3 OF 4 — Enter your institutional email.
          Type the email address where the system will send
          a 6-digit verification code.
          Click "Send Code".
  Step 5. Check your email inbox for the 6-digit OTP code.
          Enter the code in the six boxes on screen.
          Click "Verify".
  Step 6. STEP 4 OF 4 — Set your password.
          Enter a new password (at least 8 characters).
          Re-enter the same password to confirm.
          Click "Create Account".
  Step 7. You will see a success message. The system will
          automatically redirect you to your student
          dashboard.

NOTES
  - Your reference number is given by the registrar's office
    after your application has been approved.
  - If the reference number is not found, contact the
    registrar's office.
  - The OTP code expires after a short time. If it does not
    arrive, check your spam folder or request a new code.
  - Passwords must be at least 8 characters long.

[PUBLIC] — 0.3  ACCOUNT ACTIVATION (VIA EMAIL LINK)
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  Some accounts are activated by clicking a link sent to your
  email by the registrar. This page walks you through
  completing that activation.

HOW TO USE IT
  Step 1. Open your email inbox and find the activation email
          from NEMSUonePortal.
  Step 2. Click the activation link inside the email.
          Your browser will open the portal and show
          your name and program.
  Step 3. STEP 1 OF 3 — Confirm your email address.
          Type in your institutional email.
          Click "Send Code".
  Step 4. Check your email for the 6-digit OTP code.
          Enter it on screen, then click "Verify".
  Step 5. STEP 3 OF 3 — Set your password.
          Enter a new password (at least 8 characters).

  Step 6. A success screen will appear. You will be

NOTES
  - Activation links expire. If your link is expired, contact
    the registrar's office to request a new one.
  - If the link shows an error, do not try to reload.
    Contact the registrar immediately.

[PUBLIC] — 0.8  PRE-ENROLLMENT APPLICATION (NEW STUDENTS)
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  New students (freshmen and transferees) who do not yet have
  a portal account must submit a pre-enrollment application
  first. This is done through an online wizard that opens
  directly on the public landing page — no login needed.

  After submitting the wizard, the Registrar reviews the
  application. If approved, the student receives an email
  with a link to create their account and can then proceed
  with the formal online enrollment inside the portal.

  Regular students and shiftees who already have accounts
  do NOT need to go through this process — they log in
  directly and submit their enrollment from their dashboard.

BEFORE YOU START
  Prepare the following before opening the wizard:
  - A valid email address (you will receive an activation
    email at this address if your application is approved).
  - A Philippine mobile number (09XX format).
  - PDF copies of your required documents (see Step 3).

HOW TO USE IT

  Opening the Enrollment Wizard
  Step 1. Go to the portal's landing page.
  Step 2. Click any of these buttons to open the wizard:
          - "Enroll Now" (hero section)
          - "Begin enrollment" (enrollment CTA banner)
          - "Online enrollment" (footer)

  The enrollment wizard will open as a popup with 5 steps
  listed in the left panel.

  STEP 1 — Select Student Type
  Step 3. Read the four student type options and click the
          one that describes you:
          - Freshman: incoming first-year from Senior High
          - Regular: continuing student from last semester
          - Shiftee: changing programs within NEMSU Cantilan
          - Transferee: coming from another institution
  Step 4. Click "Continue".

  STEP 2 — Personal Information
  Step 5. Fill in all required fields:
          - Last name, First name, Middle name, Suffix
          - Date of birth
          - Sex (Male / Female)
          - Email address (use an address you check regularly)
          - Contact number (must be a PH mobile: 09XX XXX XXXX)
          - Program (select from the dropdown)
          - Year level (1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 4th Year)
  Step 6. Click "Continue".

  STEP 3 — Requirements Checklist
  Step 7. A list of required documents will appear based on
          your student type. Check the box next to each
          document you have ready.

          Freshman requirements:
          - SHS Form 138 / Report Card (Required)
          - PSA Birth Certificate (Required)
          - Good Moral Certificate (Required)
          - SHS Diploma / Certificate of Completion (Required)
          - 2x2 ID photos, 4 copies (Required)
          - Medical Certificate (Required)
          - Entrance Exam Result / Admission Slip (If required)
          - Accomplished Application Form (Required)

          Regular requirements:
          - Previous Certificate of Registration / COR (Required)
          - Student ID, current (Required)
          - Clearance from previous semester (Required)
          - Proof of payment or scholarship form (If applicable)

          Shiftee requirements:
          - Shifting Application Form (Required)
          - Clearance from current department (Required)
          - Dean's Endorsement Letter (Required)
          - Transcript of Records — internal (Required)
          - Acceptance from target department (Required)

          Transferee requirements:
          - Transcript of Records / TOR (Required)
          - Honorable Dismissal (Required)
          - Good Moral Certificate (Required)
          - PSA Birth Certificate (Required)
          - 2x2 ID photos, 4 copies (Required)
          - Medical Certificate (Required)
          - Entrance Exam Result (If required)
          - Application / Admission Form (Required)

  Step 8. To upload a document, click "Browse" next to the
          requirement and select a PDF file from your device.
          (Only PDF files are accepted.)
  Step 9. Click "Continue".

  STEP 4 — Subject Selection
  Step 10. A list of available subjects will appear.
           Click on a subject to select it.
           The total units will update as you select.
           Maximum is 24 units.
  Step 11. When done selecting, click "Continue".

  STEP 5 — Review & Confirm
  Step 12. Review all your information: student type,
           program, campus, academic year, and subjects.
  Step 13. If anything is wrong, click "Back" to go back
           and make corrections.
  Step 14. Click "Submit enrollment" to send your
           pre-enrollment application.

  After Submitting
  Step 15. A confirmation screen will appear.
           Your REFERENCE NUMBER will be shown
           (example: NEMSU-CAN-2026-00001).
           Write this number down or take a screenshot.
  Step 16. Bring your physical documents to the
           Registrar's Office for verification.
  Step 17. The Registrar will review your application.
           Once approved, you will receive an email
           with a link to activate your student account.
  Step 18. Click the activation link in the email
           and follow the steps to set your password.
           (See section 0.3 — Account Activation.)
  Step 19. After your account is created, log in and
           go to "Enrollment" in your dashboard to
           complete the formal enrollment process.

  For Regular and Shiftee Students
  Step 15. After reaching Step 5 and submitting, you will
           see a message: "Almost there!"
  Step 16. Click "Log in to my account" if you already
           have a portal account.
           Click "Create an account" if you need to
           register first using your reference number.
  Step 17. After logging in, go to "Enrollment" in the
           sidebar to submit your enrollment request
           for the current term.

NOTES
  - The pre-enrollment wizard is on the public landing page.
    You do not need an account or password to fill it out.
  - After submitting, you CANNOT edit your application online.
    Contact the Registrar's Office if corrections are needed.
  - The reference number is unique to your application.
    Keep it safe — you will need it to create your account.
  - Uploading documents online does NOT replace the physical
    submission. Bring the original documents to the Registrar.
  - Your pre-enrollment status (Pending, Approved, Rejected)
    is managed by the Registrar. Check your email regularly.
  - If your application is rejected, contact the Registrar
    for guidance on next steps.

[ALL ROLES] — 0.9  COMPLETE ONLINE ENROLLMENT PROCESS
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS SECTION IS FOR
  This section describes the full enrollment workflow from
  start to finish, showing what each role does and in what
  order. Use this as a reference to understand how all the
  parts of the enrollment process connect.

  Roles involved:
    - System Administrator (Admin)
    - Student
    - Registrar

----------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1 — ADMIN: Set Up the Academic Term
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Step 1. The Admin logs in and goes to
          "Academic Terms" in the sidebar.
  Step 2. Click "Add Term" (or "New Term").
  Step 3. Fill in the academic year, semester (First,
          Second, or Summer), start date, and end date.
  Step 4. Check "Is Active" to make this the current term.
  Step 5. Click "Save". The term is now created.
  Step 6. After the term is ready, click "Toggle Enrollment"
          next to the term to OPEN the enrollment window.
          (Students cannot submit requests until this is on.)

  NOTE: Only one term can be active at a time. Enrollment
  must be explicitly opened — it does not open automatically.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2 — STUDENT: Submit an Enrollment Request
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Step 7. The Student logs in and clicks "Enrollment"
          in the sidebar.
  Step 8. If enrollment is open, the Online Enrollment
          Form appears. The form has 5 steps:

    STEP 1 — Academic Term & Student Type
      a. Select the academic term to enroll in.
         (Only terms with open enrollment appear.)
      b. Select your student type:
           Freshman   — first time at this institution
           Regular    — continuing from last semester
           Shiftee    — changing programs within the school
           Transferee — coming from another institution
      c. Click "Next: Select Program".

    STEP 2 — Department & Program
      a. Select your department.
      b. Select your program from the list that appears.
      c. Click "Next: Year Level".

    STEP 3 — Year Level
      a. Select your current year level (1st–4th Year).
      b. Click "Next: Select Courses".

    STEP 4 — Select Courses
      a. The subjects for your program, year level, and
         semester will appear as a checklist.
      b. Check the box next to each subject you want.
         The total units will update automatically.
      c. (Optional) To add back subjects from other year
         levels, check "Include back courses from other
         year levels" and select the subjects you need.
      d. Click "Next: Review".

    STEP 5 — Review & Submit
      a. Review all your selections carefully:
         Term, Student Type, Department, Program,
         Year Level, selected subjects, and total units.
      b. If anything is wrong, click "Back" to correct it.
      c. Click "Submit Enrollment Request" to send your
         request to the registrar.

  Step 9. A success confirmation page will appear.
          The registrar will be notified of your request.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 3 — REGISTRAR: Review the Enrollment Request
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Step 10. The Registrar logs in and clicks
           "Enrollment Requests" in the sidebar.
  Step 11. Locate the student's request in the list.
           (Use the Status filter to show "Pending"
           requests, or search by student name/ID.)
  Step 12. Click on the request row to expand it and
           review the student's selected subjects,
           program, year level, and student type.
  Step 13. Click the "Review" button.
  Step 14. Choose a status:
           - Approved: the enrollment is accepted.
           - Rejected: the enrollment is denied.
  Step 15. (Optional) Enter remarks to explain the
           decision, especially if rejecting.
  Step 16. Click "Submit Review".

  If Approved:
  Step 17. Click "Assign Block" on the approved request.
  Step 18. Select the correct block section (e.g., Block A)
           for the student's program and year level.
  Step 19. Click "Assign". The student is now assigned
           to a block.

  NOTE: A student can only be in one block per term.
  Block capacity is managed in "Block Management".

----------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 4 — STUDENT: Receive the Decision
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Step 20. The student receives a notification about the
           outcome (Approved or Rejected).
  Step 21. The student opens "Enrollment" in the sidebar.
           At the top of the page, their enrollment status
           card shows:
           - Approved (green): fully enrolled for the term.
             The assigned block name is shown if applicable.
           - Pending (yellow): still waiting for review.
           - Rejected (red): request was denied. Any remarks
             from the registrar are shown.
  Step 22. If rejected, the student may correct the issue
           and resubmit a new request once enrollment
           is still open, or contact the registrar.

NOTES
  - A student can only submit one enrollment request
    per academic term.
  - If the enrollment window is closed before the student
    submits, they will see "Enrollment is currently closed."
    They should contact the registrar's office.
  - The full enrollment history (all past terms) is
    visible at the bottom of the student's Enrollment page.
  - Grades are only recorded after the term ends and
    faculty encode them — enrollment approval is not
    the same as having grades.

[STUDENT] — 1.1  DASHBOARD
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  The dashboard is your home page after logging in. It gives
  you a quick overview of your enrollment status, grades,
  and document requests for the current term.

HOW TO USE IT
  Step 1. Log in to the portal. You will land here
          automatically.
  Step 2. Read the summary cards at the top of the page:
          - Current Term: shows the active academic semester.
          - Enrollment Status: shows if your enrollment
            is Approved, Pending, or Rejected.

          - The bar chart shows your grades for each subject

            grouped by status.

          pages like Enrollment, Grades, Schedule, etc.
  Step 5. To sign out, click your name in the top right
          corner and select "Sign Out".

NOTES
  - The dashboard updates automatically each time you
    open the page.
  - If you see "—" in a card, it means data is not
    available yet (e.g., no grades submitted yet).

[STUDENT] — 1.2  ONLINE ENROLLMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  This page lets you submit an enrollment request for the
  current semester. The process follows a 5-step form wizard.
  Your request will be reviewed and approved by the registrar.

HOW TO USE IT

  Checking Your Current Enrollment Status
  Step 1. Open "Enrollment" from the sidebar.
  Step 2. If you are already enrolled for the current term,
          your enrollment details and status will appear
          at the top of the page (Approved, Pending, or
          Rejected).

  Submitting a New Enrollment Request
  Step 3. If enrollment is open and you have not yet
          enrolled for this term, the enrollment form
          will appear. Click through the 5 steps:

  STEP 1 — Academic Term & Student Type
    a. Select the academic term you are enrolling for.
       (Only open terms are shown.)
    b. Select your student type:
       - Freshman: first time enrolling at this institution.
       - Regular: continuing from a previous semester.
       - Shiftee: changing programs within the same school.
       - Transferee: coming from another institution.
    c. Click "Next: Select Program".

  STEP 2 — Department & Program
    a. Select your department from the list.
    b. After selecting a department, select your program
       from the programs that appear below.
    c. Click "Next: Year Level".

  STEP 3 — Year Level
    a. Select your current year level (1st, 2nd, 3rd,
       or 4th Year).
    b. Click "Next: Select Courses".

  STEP 4 — Select Courses
    a. The courses available for your program, year level,
       and semester will appear in a list.
    b. Check the box next to each subject you want to
       enroll in. The total units will update as you
       select subjects.
    c. (Optional) To add back subjects from other year
       levels, check "Include back courses from other
       year levels". Additional subjects will appear.
       Select any you need.
    d. Click "Next: Review".

  STEP 5 — Review & Submit
    a. Review all your selections: term, student type,
       program, year level, subjects, and total units.
    b. If anything is wrong, click "Back" to go back
       and make changes.
    c. When everything is correct, click
       "Submit Enrollment Request".

  Step 4. A success message will appear confirming your
          request has been submitted.
  Step 5. The registrar will review your request. You will
          be notified of the outcome.

  Viewing Your Enrollment History
  Step 6. Scroll down below the enrollment form to see
          your past enrollment requests, their statuses,
          and enrolled subjects.

NOTES
  - If enrollment is closed, you will see a message saying
    "Enrollment is currently closed." Check back later or
    contact the registrar's office.
  - You can only submit one enrollment per term.
  - If your request is rejected, you may see remarks from
    the registrar explaining why.
  - Once your enrollment is approved, you cannot change
    your subjects through this page.

[STUDENT] — 1.3  MY COURSES
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  This page shows all the subjects you are enrolled in for
  your current approved enrollment. It also shows your full
  enrollment history.

HOW TO USE IT
  Step 1. Click "My Courses" in the sidebar.
  Step 2. At the top, you will see badges showing your

          type.
  Step 3. The table below shows your enrolled subjects:
          - Course Code: the subject's code (e.g., CS101)

          - Units: number of units for that subject
          - Type: whether it is Lecture, Lab, PE, or NSTP

  Step 5. Scroll down to see your Enrollment History,
          which lists all your past enrollments with their
          statuses, programs, and subject codes.

NOTES
  - This page only shows subjects from your most recent
    approved enrollment.
  - If you have no approved enrollment, the table will
    be empty. Submit an enrollment request first.


----------------------------------------------------------------
[STUDENT] — 1.4  MY GRADES
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  This page shows your official grades for every subject
  across all academic terms. Grades are grouped by semester.

HOW TO USE IT
  Step 1. Click "My Grades" in the sidebar.
  Step 2. Your grades are displayed in sections, one per
          academic term, with the most recent term shown
          first.
  Step 3. For each term, you will see:
          - The term name (e.g., "2nd Semester 2024–2025")
          - Your Grade Weighted Average (GWA) for that term
  Step 4. The table for each term shows:
          - Subject Code
          - Subject Name
          - Units
          - Grade (numeric, e.g., 1.00 to 5.00, or special
            codes like INC for Incomplete or DRP for Dropped)
          - Remarks
  Step 5. Scroll down to see older terms.

NOTES
  - Grades only appear after your faculty has encoded and
    submitted them to the registrar.
  - If a grade shows "—", it has not been submitted yet.
  - A lower numeric grade is better (1.00 is the highest).
  - Green grades mean passing; red grades may indicate
    a failing mark (5.00) or other concern.

[STUDENT] — 1.6  DOCUMENT REQUESTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  This page lets you request official school documents, such
  as a Certificate of Enrollment, Transcript of Records, or
  Certificate of Grades. You can also track the status of
  your requests here.

HOW TO USE IT

  Submitting a New Document Request
  Step 1. Click "Document Requests" in the sidebar.
  Step 2. Click the "New Document Request" button.
  Step 3. A form will appear. Fill in the following:
          - Document Type: choose from
            * Certificate of Enrollment
            * Transcript of Records
            * Certificate of Grades
          - Purpose: briefly explain why you need it
            (e.g., "For scholarship application").
          - Number of Copies: enter how many copies you
            need (default is 1).
  Step 4. Click "Submit Request".

[FACULTY] — 2.2  GRADE ENCODING
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  This is where you enter and submit grades for the students
  in your classes. You enter midterm and final grades for each
  student, and the system calculates the final grade.

HOW TO USE IT

  Selecting a Class
  Step 1. Click "Grade Encoding" in the sidebar.
  Step 2. Click the dropdown that says "Select assignment"
          and choose the class you want to encode grades for.
          The dropdown shows: Term — Subject Code: Name (Units)
  Step 3. The list of enrolled students for that class
          will appear in a table.

  Entering Grades
  Step 4. For each student, you will see two input boxes:
          - Midterm: enter the midterm grade (1.00 to 5.00)
          - Final: enter the final grade (1.00 to 5.00)
  Step 5. As you type both grades, the "Final Grade"
          column will automatically calculate and show
          the average of midterm and final.
  Step 6. (Optional) Enter a remark in the "Remarks"

          at the top right of the student table.
  Step 10. If some students have no grades entered, the

           "Submit Anyway" button.

             to submit grades for the students that
             have grades and skip the rest.
           - Otherwise, go back and enter the missing
             grades first.
  Step 11. A green success message will confirm that
           grades have been submitted.

NOTES
  - Grades use a 1.00 to 5.00 scale. 1.00 is the highest
    and 5.00 is failing.

    cannot be edited. Contact the registrar if a
    correction is needed.
  - You must click "Save" for each student row before
    clicking "Submit All Grades". Unsaved changes are
    not included in the submission.
  - The "Final Grade" column is calculated automatically
    as the average of midterm and final grades.

WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR

     new students (freshmen/transferees) through the landing
     page, before they have a portal account.
  2. Enrollment Requests — requests submitted by existing
     students through their portal accounts.

  This page also has a third tab for Landing Page Settings.

HOW TO USE IT

  --- Tab 1: Pre-Enrollment Applications ---

  This is where you review new applicants who filled out
  the pre-enrollment wizard on the landing page.

  Step 1. Click "Enrollment Requests" in the sidebar.
  Step 2. Click the "Pre-Enrollment Applications" tab.
  Step 3. Use the Status filter to show:
          - Pending: not yet reviewed
          - Approved: application accepted
          - Rejected: application denied
  Step 4. The list shows each applicant's name, student type,
          program, year level, submission date, and status.

  Reviewing a Pre-Enrollment Application
  Step 5. Click on an applicant's row to expand it and see
          their full personal information and documents.
  Step 6. Review the uploaded documents if any were attached.
  Step 7. Click "Approve" or "Reject".
  Step 8. (Optional) Enter remarks explaining your decision.
  Step 9. Click "Submit Review".

  What happens after approval:
  - For Freshman and Transferee applicants: the system
    automatically sends an activation email to the
    student's email address. The student clicks the link
    to create their account and can then proceed with
    the formal online enrollment.
  - The application moves to "Approved" status.

  What happens after rejection:
  - The student is not sent an activation email.
  - The application moves to "Rejected" status.
  - Contact the student separately to inform them of
    the next steps.

  --- Tab 2: Enrollment Requests ---

  This tab is for students who already have portal accounts
  and submitted enrollment requests for the current term.

  Viewing Enrollment Requests
  Step 10. Click the "Enrollment Requests" tab.
  Step 11. The list shows all enrollment requests.

  Step 12. Use the Status filter to show only Pending,

  Step 13. Use the Term filter to switch between terms.
  Step 14. Use the Search bar to find a specific student
           by name or student ID.

  Reviewing a Request
  Step 15. Click on a request row to expand it and see
           the full details, including the list of
           subjects the student selected.
  Step 16. Click the "Review" button on the expanded row.
  Step 17. A form will appear:
           - Status: choose "Approved" or "Rejected".
           - Remarks: (optional) write a note to explain
             your decision, especially for rejections.
  Step 18. Click "Submit Review".
  Step 19. The student will be notified of the decision.

  Assigning a Block
  Step 20. After approving an enrollment, you can assign
           the student to a block section.
  Step 21. Click the "Assign Block" button on the
           approved request.
  Step 22. A window will appear showing available blocks
           for the student's program and year level.
           Each block shows its current enrollment count
           and capacity.
  Step 23. Select the correct block from the dropdown.
  Step 24. Click "Assign".

NOTES
  - A student can only be assigned to one block per term.
  - A block is a specific section (e.g., Block A, Block B)
    within a program and year level.
  - Approving an enrollment locks the student's subjects
    for that term.
  - If a student's enrollment request is rejected, they may
    resubmit a corrected request if enrollment is still open.
  - Pre-enrollment applications that are approved for
    freshmen/transferees automatically trigger the account
    activation email — no manual action needed.
  - If a Pending pre-enrollment application notification

          - Semester: select First, Second, or Summer.
          - Subject Code: enter the subject code
            (e.g., CS101).
          - Subject Name: enter the full subject name.
          - Units: enter the number of units (0.5–12).
          - Subject Type: choose Major or Minor.
          - Prerequisite: (optional) select a prerequisite
            subject from the dropdown.
          - Description: (optional) brief description.
  Step 5. Click "Add" to save the subject.

  Uploading Subjects in Bulk (CSV)
  Step 6. Click "Download CSV Template" to get the
          correct format for bulk upload.
  Step 7. Open the template in a spreadsheet program
          (Excel, Google Sheets) and fill in all the
          subject data.
  Step 8. Save the file as CSV format.
  Step 9. Click "Upload CSV" or drag and drop your file
          into the upload area.
  Step 10. The system will process the file and show
           a success or error message.
           - If errors are found, review the file and
             fix the issues before re-uploading.

  Editing a Subject
  Step 11. Find the subject in the subjects list.
  Step 12. Click the "Edit" button next to it.
  Step 13. Update the fields as needed.
  Step 14. Click "Save".

  Deleting a Subject
  Step 15. Click the "Delete" button next to the subject.
  Step 16. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

NOTES
  - Deleting a subject may affect existing grade records
    and enrollments that reference it. Be careful.
  - The CSV file must follow the exact template format.
    Files larger than 10 MB cannot be uploaded.
  - Prerequisites must already exist in the system
    before you can assign them.

[ADMIN] — 4.4  ACADEMIC TERMS
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  This page lets you create and manage academic terms
  (semesters). You control which term is active and whether
  enrollment is open or closed.

HOW TO USE IT

  Viewing Terms
  Step 1. Click "Academic Terms" (or "Terms") in the
          sidebar.
  Step 2. The list shows all academic terms with their
          year, semester, dates, active status, and
          enrollment open status.

  Adding a New Term
  Step 3. Click "Add Term" or "New Term".
  Step 4. Fill in the form:
          - Academic Year: enter the year (e.g., 2025).
          - Semester: select First, Second, or Summer.
          - Start Date: the term start date.
          - End Date: the term end date.
          - Is Active: check this to mark this as the
            current active term (only one can be active).
          - Enrollment Open: check this to allow students
            to submit enrollment requests.
  Step 5. Click "Save".

  Setting a Term as Active
  Step 6. Find the term in the list.
  Step 7. Click the "Set Active" button next to it.
  Step 8. Only one term can be active at a time.
          The previous active term will be deactivated.

  Opening or Closing Enrollment
  Step 9. Click the "Toggle Enrollment" button next to
          a term to open or close its enrollment window.
  Step 10. When enrollment is open, students can submit
           enrollment requests for that term.

  Editing a Term
  Step 11. Click "Edit" next to the term.
  Step 12. Update the fields as needed.
  Step 13. Click "Save".

  Deleting a Term
  Step 14. Click "Delete" next to the term.
  Step 15. Confirm the deletion.

NOTES
  - Only one term can be active at a time.
  - Closing enrollment prevents new enrollment requests
    from being submitted, but existing requests are
    not affected.
  - Do not delete a term that has existing enrollments
    or grades linked to it.

[ADMIN] — 4.5  FACULTY LOAD ASSIGNMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  This page lets you assign faculty members to subjects for a
  specific term. Faculty need to be assigned to a subject
  before they can encode grades for it.

HOW TO USE IT

  Viewing Assignments
  Step 1. Click "Faculty Load" in the sidebar.
  Step 2. Use the Term selector to choose the term.
  Step 3. The page shows:
          - Unassigned Subjects: subjects with no
            faculty assigned yet.
          - Assigned Subjects: subjects already
            linked to a faculty member.

  Assigning Faculty to a Subject
  Step 4. In the "Unassigned Subjects" section, find
          the subject you want to assign.
  Step 5. Use the Faculty dropdown next to that subject
          to select a faculty member.
  Step 6. Click "Assign".
  Step 7. The subject will move from the "Unassigned"
          section to the "Assigned" section.

  Removing an Assignment
  Step 8. In the "Assigned Subjects" section, find
          the assignment you want to remove.
  Step 9. Click "Remove" or "Delete" next to it.
  Step 10. Confirm the removal.

  Viewing a Faculty Member's Full Load
  Step 11. Click on a faculty name in the Assigned
           section to open their load detail panel.
  Step 12. The panel shows all their assignments
           across terms with subject info.

NOTES
  - Faculty cannot encode grades for a subject unless
    they are assigned to it first.
  - Each subject should be assigned to one faculty
    member per block per term.

[ADMIN] — 4.6  BLOCK MANAGEMENT

WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  The admin has full control over blocks and handles expansion
  request approvals sent by the registrar.

HOW TO USE IT

  Step 1. Click "Block Management" in the sidebar.
  Step 2. Select the Academic Term.

[ADMIN] — 4.8  AUDIT LOG
----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  The audit log records every significant action performed
  in the system — who did what, when, and from which IP
  address. Use this page for security monitoring and
  compliance checks.

HOW TO USE IT

  Viewing Audit Log Entries
  Step 1. Click "Audit Log" in the sidebar.
  Step 2. The log table shows entries with:
          - Timestamp: exact date and time of the action.
          - User: the name and role of the user.
          - Action: what was done (e.g., "login",
            "enrollment approved").
          - IP Address: the device's IP address.
          - Result: Success (green) or Failure (red).

  Filtering the Log
  Step 3. Use the Role filter to show actions by a
          specific role (e.g., only Student actions).
  Step 4. Use the Result filter to show only successful
          or failed actions.
  Step 5. Use the Date Range fields (From and To) to
          filter entries within a specific time period.
  Step 6. Type in the Action Search bar to find entries
          matching a specific action keyword.

  Expanding an Entry
  Step 7. Click on a log entry to expand it and see
          the full details and context of the action.

  Navigating Pages
  Step 8. Use the pagination controls to move between
          pages (20 entries per page).

NOTES
  - Audit logs are read-only. They cannot be edited
    or deleted by anyone.
  - Repeated failed login attempts may indicate an
    unauthorized access attempt. Investigate and lock
    the account if needed.
  - Registrar users have limited access to the audit
    log (their own actions only). The admin sees all.

WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
  This page shows the system configuration, live system state,
  and security settings. It is informational — most settings
